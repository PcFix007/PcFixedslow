Windows 10 & 11 Optimization

Boost your system performance with these proven techniques.

01 Disable Startup Apps

Slow boot times are often caused by excessive startup programs.

Ctrl + Shift + Esc > Startup tab > Right-click to Disable

02 Adjust Visual Effects

Improve system responsiveness by sacrificing some animations.

  • Search "Adjust the appearance and performance of Windows"
  • Select "Adjust for best performance"
  • Enable "Smooth edges of screen fonts" for better readability
